erik martin

community, history & rabbit holes

i help build thriving community spaces and love getting lost in historical rabbit holes.

i was reddit's first community manager and later its general manager, then led teams at depop, wework, nike, midjourney and nextdoor. these days i split my time between writing, archive research, and an independent practice in community strategy and ai readiness. the whole story →

outside work i'm obsessed with early 20th century history, especially the kind you find in vintage community cookbooks and forgotten local archives.

About This Site

hueypriest.com: plain HTML, one stylesheet, no framework, no tracker.

The bands running across the page are cross-stitch X's drawn as inline SVG. The background is aida cloth, the mesh you stitch onto, rendered as two repeating gradients. The red came off a photo of a garage sign. The lettering is borrowed from 1970s science-fiction paperbacks.

Built the way the old church cookbooks were: by hand, for a small audience, with the sources kept.
