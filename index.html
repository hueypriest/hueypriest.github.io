About This Site

hueypriest.com — one HTML file, no framework, no tracker, no build step.

The bands running across the page are cross-stitch X's drawn as inline SVG. The background is aida cloth, the mesh you stitch onto, rendered as two repeating gradients. The red came off a photo of a garage sign.

Built the way the old church cookbooks were: by hand, for a small audience, with the sources kept.
